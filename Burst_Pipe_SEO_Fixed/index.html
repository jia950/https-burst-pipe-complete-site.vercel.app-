How it works

Clear guidance before you arrange service

When a pipe problem appears, it can be hard to know what matters first. Use these steps to prepare and ask the right questions.

  • Describe the issue. Note where the water is coming from and whether it is still flowing.
  • Take basic safety steps. Keep away from electrical hazards and shut off water only if the valve is safely accessible.
  • Confirm the provider. Ask about provider qualifications, availability, costs and the scope of work.
